# Setting Boundaries That Stick: A 5-Day Challenge

## Course Info

- **Instructor:** Melody Wilding
- **Duration:** 16m (5 days × 3-5 min videos)
- **Level:** Beginner + Intermediate
- **Rating:** 4.7 (92)

## Learning Objectives

- Apply boundary-setting techniques in hybrid & async work
- Build consistent boundary routine through small daily decisions
- Use practical scripts across Slack, email, meetings

## Skills Covered

- Self-confidence
- Personal Boundaries
- Personal Development

## Day 1: Solve Your Biggest Boundary Block

- **Goal:** Pick your first boundary focus
- **Avoid high-stakes situations first**
- **Two Filters**
  - Cost: mental load + calendar time
  - Political Risk: chance of damaging trust
- **Target:** High cost + lowest political risk
- **Neutralize Fear: Worst/Best/Most Likely**
  - Worst case: they get upset
  - Best case: they agree, you gain time
  - Most likely: a little awkward, then fine

## Day 2: Stop the Knee-Jerk "Yes" Habit

- **Goal:** Install a pattern interrupt
- **Why we overcommit**
  - Caught off guard (Teams ping, hallway ask)
  - Self-created urgency (10pm message)
- **Deferral Scripts**
  - From others: "Let me check what's possible..."
  - From yourself: "This feels urgent, but..."
- **Somatic Resets**
  - Unclench jaw, drop tongue
  - Hand on stomach, breathe
  - Take a lap, grab water
- **Task:** Pick one somatic reset to try

## Day 3: Renegotiate a Commitment

- **Key Insight:** No is not a complete sentence at work
- **Tactic 1: Trade-Off Approach**
  - Problem-solving discussion
  - What to pause, deprioritize, slow down
  - Example: "We'll push out X to make room"
- **Tactic 2: Conditional Yes**
  - Agree now, set limits for future
  - Great for banking social capital
  - Example: "I can this time, but not always..."
- **Both tactics offer a way forward**
  - Protect credibility + bandwidth

## Day 4: Hold Firm When Others Push Back

- **Risk:** Caving into over-explaining
- **Formula: Acknowledge-Restate-Redirect**
  - Acknowledge: show you heard them
  - Restate: boundary is still in place
  - Redirect: shift to workable solution
- **Example: Last-minute meeting**
  - "I know ideally I'd be there live..."
  - "I can send input in advance..."
- **Example: Unrealistic deadline**
  - "I'm invested in this going well..."
  - "Let's adjust the scope slightly..."
- **Practice anywhere:** work, home, partner, kid

## Day 5: Set Digital Boundaries

- **Core Principle:** Expectations beat boundaries
- **Have the conversation**
  - Define turnaround times
  - Identify urgent projects/clients
  - State your limits clearly
- **Bonus Moves**
  - Add working hours to email signature
  - Step up status updates ("deep work, back at 2pm")
  - Confirm receipt after hours ("got it, Monday")
- **Set boundaries with yourself**
  - Fully turn off devices at night
  - Create friction to prevent slip-back
  - Have something to look forward to

## Conclusion

- Tools for balance and focus
- Interrupt knee-jerk yes
- Renegotiate scope, hold firm, protect time off
- Come back when patterns creep back
