# Market Research: Anthropic

## Executive Summary

Anthropic, the AI safety company behind the Claude model family, has achieved a historic IPO in November 2026, raising over $81 billion—the largest IPO on record. The company has evolved from a 2021 startup to a $1.5 trillion valuation powerhouse, with aggressive expansion in compute infrastructure, enterprise adoption, and AI ecosystem building.

**Key Takeaways:**
- **IPO Status**: Successfully listed on Nasdaq (ticker: ANT) in November 2026
- **IPO Proceeds**: $81.3 billion at $83/share, 980M shares sold
- **Post-IPO Valuation**: $1.5 trillion
- **2025 Revenue**: $4.58 billion (1,368% YoY growth in Q2 2026)
- **Cash Position**: $95.6B cash + $36.3B short-term investments
- **Total Debt**: $41.79B (as of Dec 2025) + ~$900B future commitments

### 4.2 Bond Market

Anthropic has primarily raised capital through:
- **Equity financing** (VC rounds + IPO)
- **Revolving credit facilities** (bank lending)
- **Strategic term sheet commitments** (Amazon, Google)

Unlike traditional tech companies, Anthropic has not issued public bonds or corporate debt securities to retail investors. All debt is held by institutional financial institutions.
